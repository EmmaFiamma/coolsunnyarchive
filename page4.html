The garden after rain

painting 2026

"Somewhere between memory and weather, a garden keeps what the rain forgot."

This piece explores quiet places, half remembered landscapes and invisible connections between nature and memory.

A study of blue light, water, old stories and places that may exist only somewhere between dreams and reality.
